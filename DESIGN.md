## Implementation choices under delegated direction

- Keep the existing IFUIX logo and warm orange identity. Use warm paper backgrounds, dark readable text, and a deeper orange for accessible actions. Garden green remains meaningful on Kebun Pintar pages.
- Use the system sans-serif stack for readable Indonesian/English and no remote font request. Larger headline scale establishes hierarchy; normal prose remains readable rather than tightly tracked.
- Static home product shot, with real Framix screenshot and an explicit demo destination. Product rows show real screens, features, availability, and specific actions. Fuira screenshot selection is manual, without automatic rotation.
- Home, product directory, founder story, services examples, and practical contact information have different layouts. Lines group actual sections; no decorative grid, glow, background blobs, or repeated floating cards.
- Use icons for functional controls or real product features, not decorative sparkle badges. Existing product galleries retain keyboard-operable dialogs and videos.
- Energy 2, rhythm 3, motion 1: product imagery and orange supply character; layouts vary with content; no automatic carousel or entrance animation is needed.
- LinkedIn appears in About, Contact, and the shared footer using the existing profile URL. No external profile claims beyond the owner's confirmation and stored link.
- Contact uses direct email and WhatsApp destinations. The old FormSubmit form is replaced so the website no longer routes personal messages through an unverified submission setup.

These are assistant design decisions under the owner's brief, not invented biographical statements. Owner visual review remains the final acceptance step.
