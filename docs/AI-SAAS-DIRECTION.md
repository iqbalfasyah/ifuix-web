## IFUIX Finance concept

Working name, not trademark-checked or final branding. Audience: freelancers and small businesses organizing operational finances. Problem: records spread across receipts/invoices and manual categories, making cash-flow visibility difficult. AI is integral to extracting transaction drafts, proposing classifications, summarizing confirmed records and answering questions with source references.

Proposed MVP: upload receipt/invoice or import CSV; extracted amount/date/vendor/category shown for confirmation; transaction list and corrections; income/expense dashboard; unpaid invoice view; period summaries; questions grounded in the user's confirmed records; export/delete account data. No bank connection, money movement, tax filing, investment recommendation or autonomous transaction approval in the initial concept. Extraction and arithmetic should be evaluated separately; deterministic code calculates totals, AI explains grounded results, uncertain fields require correction.

Architecture proposal: authenticated web SaaS, isolated account records, consent-based document handling, structured model responses validated before preview, deterministic money calculations and traceable references. Provider/model, retention, encryption/access controls, pricing, billing and hosting are decisions for implementation, not delivered promises. Evaluate wrong amounts/categories, missing documents, prompt injection in uploads, unsupported answers, deletion/export and account isolation before launch. No bank or user-finance data imported in this task. No deadline or fabricated customers. CTA is WhatsApp enquiry, no database waitlist.
